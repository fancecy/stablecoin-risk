Interventions to compare
Fixed parameters and assumptions

60% of baseline net cash is frozen and released on freeze duration + 1. Market-value shock: 0%; liquidation haircut: 1%; horizon: 30 days. Requests arrive over five days with weights of 40/25/15/10/10%.

The prepositioned cash target is 30% of initial liabilities, prepared 30 days before the shock, with a 4% annual opportunity spread. Extra cash is assumed accessible outside the frozen channel.

Credit is capped at 10% of initial liabilities, with 8% annual interest, a 0.5% annual commitment fee, and collateral equal to 110% of principal. The facility is assumed precommitted. No repayment is modelled within the horizon; interest remains payable.

The sales cap uses original Treasury holdings. Only unpledged assets can be sold. The cash buffer is prepared before the shock, independently of credit activation.

Scope of interpretation

This is a balance-sheet simulation under explicit assumptions, not causal policy evaluation or price prediction. Reserve publication timing is unverified, and direct liquidation or collateral rights are not established. Confidence feedback, endogenous runs, and insolvency resolution are not modelled. Observed prices and simulated paths are separate.
